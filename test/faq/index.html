Getting in

Which phones can play?

Android 7.1 or newer.

Is it free?

Yes. There is nothing to buy.

Will the Group fill my inbox?

Only if you want it to. Choose every post, a daily digest or no email in the Group's settings.

Playing

Why 14 days?

Google Play asks every new game for a 14-day test before launch. Stay in the test for the 14 days and the game gets there.

Do I have to play every day?

No. A two-minute town on most days is plenty.

How do I say what I think?

Post in the Group: what's fun, what isn't, anything that looks broken.

How does my name get in the credits?

By name or nickname, your choice. We'll ask which once you're in.

Good to know

Why are there ads in a test?

Test builds show Google's test ads. They only play when you pick one for a reward.

What does the game do with my data?

The privacy policy lists it all.

Privacy policy

Can I leave?

Any time, from the same link you used to become a tester.
