Bloom Dozer privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Bloom Dozer is a game by Flintglass Games ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. Questions or requests: flintglassgames@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress and settings on your device. We don't receive them. They are deleted when you uninstall the game.

Your age

The first time you play, the game asks your age. The answer stays on your device: the game only uses it to decide which kind of ads you get (see below) and whether to ask about play stats.

Ads

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: rewarded ads you choose to watch for a reward, and a short ad between some towns. When an ad loads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (to estimate your general location), device information, how you interact with the ad, and diagnostics. It uses them to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Players under 13, and players who don't give an age, only see non-personalized ads suitable for children. Players under 16 are never asked to agree to personalized ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), Google's consent form asks before any ads are personalized, and SETTINGS > AD PRIVACY lets you change your answer at any time. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Play stats

If you say yes to PLAY STATS (asked once, and changeable in SETTINGS), the game sends anonymous gameplay events, such as which towns you finish, to Unity Analytics to help us improve the game. Players under 13 are never asked, and nothing is sent without a yes. Turning it off later also asks Unity to delete what was sent.

Sharing

We don't sell your information. The only data that leaves the game is what Google collects for ads and, with your yes, the play stats described above.

Security and retention

Data sent to Google and Unity is encrypted in transit. We keep no data of our own about you. For data they hold, see their policies, or email us and we will help you make a request.

Children

We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The age screen at first launch gives players under 13 child-safe ads only and never asks them for play stats.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the new version at this address.
